Light in Every Step

نور في كل خطوةEvery step is a step towards light.

Madinah’s name means “the enlightened city”, and the Prophet’s footsteps lit it. I built the whole Ramadan story on that: each guest moment, from arrival to Suhoor to the view of Al-Masjid Al-Nabawi, became one step of a journey of light, told with French art de vivre and never stated outright.

My role

Result: Best Campaign of the Year 2026 at the Hotelier Saudi Awards, and kept by the hotel as its Ramadan platform for three years.

Healthcare and pharma · concepts, rationales and activations

Medical stories people actually feel

In pharma the idea has to move an audience and survive medical review. Every route below starts from the science or the patient insight, says why it works, and ends in something an HCP or a woman can do.

Keytruda women’s oncology · EIOC 2026 congress

One story across four women’s cancers

The brief: a 360° congress experience introducing Keytruda’s new ovarian cancer indication, while telling the bigger story of four women’s cancers (breast, cervical, endometrial, ovarian), keeping each indication distinct and medically credible. The new subcutaneous form takes 1 minute every 3 weeks or 2 minutes every 6 weeks, according to the prescribed treatment plan.

Concept 1The Butterfly EffectOne small change. A different world.Appeals: Hope, transformation, empowerment

Rationale. Lorenz’s butterfly effect: a tiny change can trigger a massive outcome. A one-minute subcutaneous injection is that small change in the treatment experience. The ovary’s shape echoes a butterfly, and its four wings carry the four indications: breast, cervical, endometrial, ovarian.

Concept 2Take Back Your TimeMore moments for life.Appeals: Empowerment, freedom, storytelling

Rationale. Cancer makes time feel like it belongs to treatment. One minute, according to the prescribed plan, gives it back: a daughter’s school play, a family dinner, a work milestone. The desire for normal life becomes believable.

Concept 3Your Key to LifeUnlock more possibilities.Appeals: Hope, possibility, emotional connection

Rationale. The brand name holds a key. The ankh, the ancient Egyptian symbol of life, is a key too. Together they unlock the idea, then hand over to the science: behind the symbol is evidence, mechanism and progress.

Concept 4Roots of HopeYour roots. Your strength. Your journey.Appeals: Emotional connection, resilience, science

Rationale. Cancer shows at the surface but its biology runs deeper. Pembrolizumab acts on an underlying mechanism, immune evasion, not only what is visible. Roots carry a double meaning: the biology beneath the disease and the people who keep a woman grounded.

NewBridge × Lundbeck · neuropsychiatry regional event · 2026

Nine ways to show what the brain can’t

In November 2025 NewBridge Pharmaceuticals took over Lundbeck’s commercial operations across the Middle East and North Africa. For their regional stand-alone event on depression, bipolar disorder and schizophrenia, I planned the event concept from the first idea to the booth: nine creative territories, each with its own slogan, rationale, visual and activation, so the client could choose the story.

1 · InsightThe brain is a network; these disorders are about broken connections, and NewBridge’s name is a bridge.
2 · TerritoryNine metaphors for connection: cosmic web, light, puzzle, prism, data, planets, mind trap, maze, harmony.
3 · Line and visualOne slogan and one key visual per route, written to stay scientific, never promising outcomes.
4 · Event executionExample: the Lundbeck star appears at the entrance, then branches into lit pathways leading to each scientific booth.

Activations I planned for the event

The star entranceLundbeck’s star greets guests at the entrance, then branches into lit pathways that lead to each scientific booth. Every path is one part of the programme, and all return to brain health.
The cosmic web installationA monumental suspended network of illuminated threads and nodes; regions light up as each scientific topic is introduced.
Disorder stationsDepression, bipolar disorder and schizophrenia each get their own illuminated station on the shared network, so HCPs move from the big picture into each condition.
The prism walkA glass prism splits one beam into three coloured corridors, one per disorder, so each disorder is explored on its own path.

The psychology behind the creative

I studied media to understand how stories are told. I studied psychology to understand why they matter.

Before I ask what a brand should say, I ask what its audience needs to feel, understand or remember. That is how a Ramadan campaign became a journey of light, and how a breast-health brief became a conversation between women.

Emotional storytellingBeyond what a brand offers, to what the experience means to the person receiving it.
Audience insightPast demographics, to what makes a message feel relevant, trusted and worth remembering.
Memory and nostalgiaThe associations that make a palace, a city or a season feel like yours.
Empathy in sensitive subjectsHealthcare and faith handled with respect, never with fear.
